Student Welfare & Safety Guidelines

Document ID: SWMS-ST-2026-v1 | Effective Date: Fall Semester 2026 | Scope: State Board Hostels

1. Code of Conduct & Hostel Rules

Enrolled student residents must maintain community discipline, uphold campus safety protocols, and show respect to co-residents, hostel staff, and the governing warden.

1.1 General Residence Rules

  • Room damage, property alteration, or unauthorized furniture relocation is strictly prohibited.
  • Maintaining room sanitation and hygiene is the responsibility of the allocated occupants.
  • Possession of unapproved electrical heaters, heavy appliances, or illegal substances is a primary offense and results in immediate boarding cancellation.

2. Mandatory Study & Curfew Hours

Academic discipline is a cornerstone of the student residence program. All hostels observe standard silent study schedules.

2.1 Daily Study Hours

Every resident must observe the mandatory study slot from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM, Sunday through Thursday. During these hours, students are expected to remain in study rooms or their allocated quarters. Recreational noise, television, or outdoor sports are strictly prohibited.

2.2 Gate Curfew

Hostel outer gates are locked promptly at 9:30 PM daily. Residents returning after curfew must produce parent-authorized written consent or warden pre-clearance to enter.

3. Mess Schedules & Dining Standards

Hostel dining spaces operate on a strict nutritional standard code with scheduled dining timings.

3.1 Dining Timings

  • Breakfast: 7:30 AM – 8:45 AM
  • Lunch: 12:30 PM – 2:00 PM
  • Dinner: 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM

3.2 Dining Rules

Students must queue and present their physical ID card / room receipt. Wastage of food is subject to administrative warnings. Outside food storage or catering is not permitted inside dining areas.

4. Anti-Ragging & Medical Safety

The state has a strict zero-tolerance policy against any form of bullying, harassment, or ragging.

4.1 Zero-Tolerance Policy

Any act of mental or physical abuse, severe practical jokes, or coercion towards new student arrivals is classified as a criminal offense. Violators will face immediate hostel expulsion and legal police prosecution.

4.2 First Aid & Illness Reporting

If a resident falls ill, they must notify the floor representative or warden immediately. Each hostel maintains standard first-aid cupboards and is mapped to local ambulance dispatch centers.
