Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to standard procedures, account credentials, and platform security access.

How do I register a new warden account?
Warden accounts are pre-authorized by the State Welfare Administrator. Go to the Warden Login page, select 'Create Account', and enter your pre-enrolled username and mobile number to complete password configuration and security registration with Google Authenticator.
Is Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) mandatory?
Yes. Under state safety compliance rules, all administrative portal logins require a verified 6-digit TOTP token generated via Google Authenticator or registered security devices. This is enforced from June 2026.
How do I modify student check-in records?
Authenticated wardens can access the Student Management tab, search the student roster, select student records, and update room allocations, contact details, or check-out statuses directly from the dashboard.
Who do I contact if I lose my mobile authenticator device?
If you cannot access your MFA token, contact Nithin at the State Portal Support Navigator desk at 8978771345 or submit a recovery ticket to support@swms.gov. Our team will verify your identity and issue a new MFA configuration key.
What data is stored in the SWMS portal?
SWMS securely stores student roll numbers, class details, parent contact information, room assignments, and hostel occupancy records. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Warden credentials are hashed using BCrypt and never stored in plain text.
