Our Vision

Supporting Education, Ensuring Welfare

This system serves as a central registry to collect student data from authorized hostel wardens. The primary goal is to ensure that welfare programs, food provisions, and accommodations are distributed accurately to students without any administrative overhead.

Administrators at the District and State level utilize the system to view reports, audit system actions, reset accounts, and monitor hostels dynamically.

  • Clean Child-Friendly Interface Theme
  • Role-Based Dashboards and Permissions
  • Interactive Reports and PDF/Excel Exports
SWMS
