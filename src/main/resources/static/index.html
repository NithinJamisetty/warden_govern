How the SWMS Portal Works

Watch our quick video guide explaining warden enrollment, MFA security registration, and student data audits.

1. Warden Self-Registration

Wardens pre-authorized by District Admins complete registration by scanning their secure two-factor auth QR code and setting their password.

2. Dynamic Student Roster

Wardens manage hostel student roll numbers, mobile numbers, classes, and track amenities. Updates reflect instantly on District charts.

3. Administrative Oversight

Super Admins and District Admins audit logins, monitor active wardens, trace actions via audit logs, and download aggregated reports.

Ensuring Administrative Transparency

SWMS bridges the gap between state education departments, district supervisors, and hostel wardens on the ground.

Supporting Student Education & Welfare

The Student Welfare Management System is designed to record student attendance, manage hostel room inventories, track meal supplies, and record amenities provided by the government.

Our digital system allows district administrators to view real-time enrollment analytics, preventing administrative delays and ensuring that resources are sent directly to the students who need them.

Welfare Mission

Provide clean, secure, and supportive housing environments for underprivileged students to nurture academic excellence and career success.

Two-Factor Security

Protects students' personal records and government accounts from unauthorized logins using strong TOTP security policies.

Transparent Audit Logs

Keeps administrators informed about changes made to student rolls or configurations with fully non-repudiable audit logs.
